                  Frequently asked questions

                  Ordering

                  How do I place an order?

                  Open the Menu, press “Add to cart” on any dish, then open the cart and press “Proceed to checkout”.

                  Can I change my order after placing it?

                  Yes, while the order is “Received” or “Preparing” you can cancel it from the Track Order page and order again, or call us.

                  Delivery

                  How long does delivery take?

                  Usually 30–40 minutes. The Track Order page shows a live estimate.

                  How much is delivery?

                  Rs 150 per order, and free for orders over Rs 2,000.

                  Payment

                  Which payment methods do you accept?

                  Cash on delivery, and Visa or Mastercard debit/credit cards.

                  Is my card information safe?

                  Card payments are processed securely and we never store your full card number.
